CSS Expression Grammar
======================

This grammar describes the expression subset currently implemented by the
values parser. Whitespace may occur between any two components unless stated
otherwise.

expression
    ::= additive-expression

additive-expression
    ::= multiplicative-expression
        ((PLUS | MINUS) multiplicative-expression)*

multiplicative-expression
    ::= primary-expression
        ((STAR | SLASH) primary-expression)*

primary-expression
    ::= NUMBER
      | PERCENTAGE
      | DIMENSION
      | parenthesized-expression

parenthesized-expression
    ::= PAREN_OPEN expression PAREN_CLOSE


Operator precedence
-------------------

1. Parenthesized expressions
2. Multiplication and division
3. Addition and subtraction

Operators at the same precedence level associate from left to right.


Supported value forms
---------------------

- Numeric literals
- Percentages
- Dimensions with units
- Parenthesized expressions
- The operators +, -, *, and /
- var() substitution before expression parsing

Nested functions other than var() are not currently accepted as expression
primaries. Functions such as min(), max(), and clamp() are therefore outside
this grammar until explicitly implemented.
